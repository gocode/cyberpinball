# What's new in Cyber Pinball

One section per release, `## <version>` then one `- ` line per item, translating the same
section of [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) point by point (same versions, same number of items).
`npm run publier` puts both languages in the update feed, where the cabinet shows the notes in
its own language before installing, and in the GitHub release. Short sentences, written for the cabinet's user.

## 0.1.7

- Backups, in System menu > Cyber Pinball: copy your settings and scores, your tables too, or the whole installation, to a USB drive, a disk or a network drive, at its root or in the folder of your choice ("Choose a folder…"). Only changed files are copied again, and every file written is read back. A level that does not fit on the drive is greyed out with its reason (not enough space, FAT32 drive and a file over 4 GB).
- During a backup the menu stays free, but tables wait for the copy to end. A copy that was stopped, or whose drive was removed, keeps what was verified: "Resume the backup" only copies what is missing.
- Restore: a plan first shows, part by part, what will be replaced, put back or kept, and Start includes or excludes each part. Cyber Pinball restarts and puts the files in place before reading them; the previous state goes to the trash, and "Undo the last restore" brings it back. A backup from another cabinet does not take its screens or its identity on the friends network by default.
- Automatic backup (off by default): settings and scores every day or every week, "As soon as possible" (the first time the cabinet is free during the day or the week, since it is not on all day) or "At a set time" (the chosen time and day; if missed, as soon as possible). A game started takes priority: the backup stops and resumes at the next free moment. Kept on the cabinet (the last 10) then copied to the drive as soon as it is plugged in.
- Screen layout: on the screens other than the panel's, the mouse gesture reminder moves to the hint line at the bottom and no longer covers the label of the top zone. Guest mode: each padlock stays in front of its text. The file drag-and-drop prompts now show in English when the interface is.
- LED and feedback (DOF), in System menu > Borne: Cyber Pinball recognises the cabinet's controllers (Pinscape, LedWiz, Teensy, DudesCab, PacLed…) without sending them anything, turns on the VPX DOF plugin, and says what keeps an LED from lighting up: no configtool configuration, a controller without its file, an undeclared Teensy. "Ouvrir configtool.vpuniverse.com" opens the site in "Récupérer": the downloaded ZIP comes back and is imported, the previous configuration is backed up.
- Addressable LEDs (WS2812): describe your strips (Teensy or Wemos D1 controller, port, outputs, rows and columns, wiring, colours, brightness) and Cyber Pinball writes the DOF Cabinet.xml, never replacing a hand-written file. Also over Art-Net (WLED on an ESP32, 170 LEDs per universe); for a DudesCab or a Pinscape Pico, the screen explains that their strips are described in the board's tool. The screen points out that a KL25Z does not drive addressable LEDs. A table's doctor reports a ROM without DOF effects or a table without PinMAME, and the "Essentiels" tab of the VPX settings picks, for the contactors, knocker or shaker, the table sound, the cabinet or both.
- Add a table > “From another installation…”: the PinUP Popper or PinballX tables, listed one by one; tick the ones you want, and each arrives with its PuP-Pack, its ROM, its colours and sounds, its DMD folder, its backglass and its media. A table chosen alone in the Tables folder brings them too. The plan says what was found and what is missing; the original installation does not change.
- Game parts: “Look for a file or a folder…” also takes a folder (“Take this whole folder”), a PuP-Pack for instance. In a folder that holds several, such as the whole PUPVideos, the table's own is taken alone, or chosen from the list.

## 0.1.6

- Game with friends on a table whose DMD is drawn by its script (FlexDMD, like The Goonies): VPX no longer stops mid-game. Cyber Pinball read that DMD during the game in a way that could damage VPX's memory; it now only reads the DMD of ROM tables (the friends' scores still go on its score screen, on the backglass for the others). If VPX stops anyway, the screen says why (the table's script error, line and message, or exit code explained) and Start plays the table again, or opens the game with friends, instead of looking for what is missing to play.
- Notifications on every screen: the number of friends online and every notification now show in the system menu and each of its sub-screens, the help, messages and attract mode, not only on the wheel; over the built-in browser too. An invitation opens by itself over the open screen, and "Answer later" gives it back as it was. During a game: no counter, only the invitation, a friend coming online and the game with friends. Invitation endings reach both cabinets: a refusal shows right away at the top of the room (their seat freed), a cancellation, an expiry or a friend gone offline changes the invitation's screen ("Invitation cancelled by Bruno"), and the message is sent again if it gets lost.
- Game with friends: several games in a row each start, and original tables such as The Goonies or The Matrix no longer stay on "Loading table…": their "ready" is read before the game. A table on screen that does not say it is ready is ready anyway after 20 s, and the log says why. An invitation or an answer lost on the way is sent again.
- Notifications during games: a friend coming online, an invitation ("Bruno invites you to join a game") and news of a game with friends now show all the time, also in the system menu and at the top of the playfield during games, over VPX without taking control from it, with their sound. A friend coming online has their own little sound, at most once a quarter of an hour. System menu > Friends > "Hide notifications during games" turns them off during games: everything then waits for the menu.
- Dedicated cabinet on Windows Pro and Home, and launch at startup: System menu > Cabinet > "Dedicated cabinet" (or the new "Launch at startup") offers two ways, your choice. "Replace the desktop for this account" starts the session straight in Cyber Pinball, with no desktop or taskbar, and restarts it if it closes (Shell Launcher does not exist on Pro: the option offered nothing). "Launch at Windows startup" opens it with Windows, the desktop hidden behind it (black background, no icons). Before writing, each Windows setting it changes is backed up; "Turn off" puts it back as it was and checks it, and a rescue script does the same outside Cyber Pinball.
- Game with friends: on automatic, the others' scores strip goes on the backglass, even when the table uses it (B2S, PuP-Pack): discreet, at the bottom, always in front. Without a backglass, on the topper; with neither, at the bottom of the playfield. "Scores on the DMD" becomes a separate setting, on by default: the scores show there at quiet moments, and not at all once it is off.
- Game with friends: the others' scores really show on the DMD, every 12 s during play, on the table's score screen (never during an animation, the BONUS, a choice to make or initials entry); with the ball in play they almost never did. On a FlexDMD table (The Goonies), whose image is no longer read, they show at the same pace, with the ball in play. The log says, for each game, how many passes there were, or why none. The strip's cards have a slightly more opaque background: it stands out better from an animated PuP-Pack.
- The built-in browser blocks ads: on YouTube (before and during videos), in search results and on the pages you look at before downloading a media file. The table forums are never filtered, so signing in and downloading there work as before. "Bloquer les publicités" (Block ads) setting in Site accounts, on by default; the filter lists are updated every day.
- The cabinet no longer always opens on the same table: in "Médias, son et attente" (Media, sound and attract mode), "Table au démarrage" (Table at startup) picks the first one, a random table or the last one played, and "Ordre des tables" (Table order) sorts the wheel's medallions by title, last played, most played, year, manufacturer, or in an order shuffled at every start.
- System menu > Tables > "Toutes les tables" (All tables): the list of all your tables; Start opens the table's page, the same as from the wheel (Play, Edit, media, parts, files, settings…), and its "Retour" (Back) goes back to the list.
- Sharing and network: "Limiter le débit des téléchargements…" (Limit download speed…) caps what your friends send you (from 256 KB/s to 20 MB/s, or no limit), all transfers together, to keep the connection free for the rest of the house. A friend whose cabinet is not up to date still sends at full speed.
- The volume buttons now really set the sound of the whole cabinet (the Windows volume: VPX, PinMAME, PuP, the menu), in the menu as well as during a game: 5% per press, and Volume − held for one second mutes or restores the sound. A "Muet" (Mute) button can also be captured in the "Boutons" (Buttons) step. A badge shows the level at the top of the playfield. Before, they only moved VPX's mix, by 1% per press, with no audible effect.
- Cyber Pinball no longer closes by itself when Windows briefly refuses to save a file (antivirus, indexing): the save is retried, and if it still fails, it is noted in the log and done again at the next one. During a game with friends, the cabinet no longer restarted and no longer went offline for the others.
- Friends: presence no longer flickers. A friend stays online as long as a link connects them to your cabinet, over the Internet or the local network, and a link that drops is remade without taking them offline (15 s of grace); both cabinets see each other the same way. A cabinet turned on at the same time as another finds it within a minute, no longer after several minutes, even when one of them blocks incoming connections. "Seen" dates the last moment the friend was really online.
- Friends: the list says whether it can be trusted. "Looking for friends…" at startup, "Updating…" when a link just dropped, then "List up to date: 1 friend online"; in "My friends", "Play with a friend", the system menu and the table menu pill. A friend's sheet says how they are linked (Internet, local network).
- Game with friends: "Go!" always fades after 4 s, even on a table without live tracking or when the connection goes quiet (it could stay on screen). A "ready" or a "go" lost during a cut is sent again: nobody waits for the delay for nothing any more.
- Game with friends: a table without live tracking of the game state is marked "Manual start, no live score", with the reason, when choosing the table, in the room and in the invitation. Its score counts if it enters the table's high score table; otherwise the results say why it is missing. An error after the game no longer loses its result.
- Getting a table from a friend: the parts screen opens in the list, on the first part, and each line says "Ticked" or "Unticked". The ROM and the PuP-Pack say how to take them: Start on their line, then the declaration of your rights; once ticked, you are back on the ROM's line, ticked. The flippers go down from the list to "Get the n ticked parts", then to "Back".
- A transfer from a friend no longer starts twice. While it runs, waits for your friend or is not installed, the table shows it in their library ("Transfer in progress", its bar, its percentage) and on its page ("See the table transfer"); Start leads to it instead of starting it again. The transfer screen says it has started and carries on if you leave it; the friend's page counts their transfers in progress.
- In the built-in browser, a link clicked a second time while it is downloading does not start again: "Already in progress".
- Getting a table from a friend: every unticked part says it is available and how to tick it; the PuP-Pack is taken like the ROM, with Start and the declaration of your rights.
- A table received from a friend says "Received, not installed yet" and offers first "Install in my library", on the transfer screen as in Downloads, instead of "Back to …'s library".
- A friend's table no longer stops at a refused media file (such as a ".png" that is really a JPEG): such a file is no longer announced, and a file refused on the way is skipped with its real reason ("media refused by the checks: …") while the rest of the table arrives. "No longer shares it" is no longer shown wrongly.
- On the cabinet, a screen's actions follow its content, right below it, instead of staying stuck at the bottom of the screen under a large gap.
- Windows Firewall: the cabinet network and VPX rules are checked at every startup and right after every update, and set again on their own, with nothing to confirm, if one is missing. Windows' permission is needed only once, from "Allow in Windows Firewall": after an update, the "Windows Firewall" screen offers it if needed, never in the middle of a game.
- Windows Firewall: a rule Windows blocks on its own (its "Allow access" prompt left unanswered) is no longer taken for a permission. "Sharing and network" says "Cyber Pinball blocked" with the reason, and "My friends" says "Cabinet network blocked by Windows Firewall" instead of showing your friends offline.
- Startup intro: when Cyber Pinball opens, the playfield, backglass, topper, DMD and FullDMD play the Cyber Pinball animation together, then keep their last frame: "Press a button to choose a table", and the first button shows the wheel. On the playfield, the ball leaves the plunger, takes the orbit and hits the target, and the logo settles in. In "Startup intro", play it or turn it off, "Watch the intro", and put your own image or video on each screen and your own background music; going back to the Cyber Pinball animations deletes nothing.

## 0.1.5

- Game with friends: when choosing the table, the ones your friends have in the same version come first. The others stay visible, greyed out with the reason and the name of who blocks ("Other version at Bruno’s", "Bruno does not have this table"); Start on another version opens theirs in their library, to get it. No more refusal at the invitation because of a different version. With several friends, "Change table" in the room and "Another table, same players" on the results.
- Game with friends: when you are not on the game's screen, a one-line notification says right away that a guest accepts ("Bruno and Léa accepted") or declines, and why, that a player leaves the game, or that the game is over for everyone (the winner, your rank, where to see the results). On the tables menu, in the system menu, and over a game on another table if you finished before the others; back in the menu, the results open by themselves. Even with notifications off.
- Game with friends: everyone starts at the same time. Each cabinet waits until every table has loaded; while waiting, a card in the middle of the playfield says who is awaited and how far along they are (“Waiting for Bruno — Loading table…”), then “Go!” on every cabinet. A player who is not ready after 90 s no longer holds the others up: they start as soon as their table is. A cabinet left on an old version is flagged and not awaited.
- On a desktop, the backglass behind the table menu now shows a video too: a table whose backglass is a video kept the previous image, or even the previous table's, while the backglass screen showed the video.
- Tables whose script calls vpmInit twice, such as Back to the Future (Data East 1990), now start: VPX 10.8 stopped them at every launch ("Table script bug: cvpmFlips2.Init is called twice"). Cyber Pinball plays a fixed copy of their script, without modifying the table, from installation and before each game; if a game still stops on that message, the script is fixed for the next one.
- Game history with friends (System menu > Friends > "Game history"): every finished live game is kept on the cabinet, newest first, the table as the title and the players below with their rank and score. Open a game to review its details: table version, each player's time, why a score was not ranked.
- Game with friends: a cabinet left on an old Cyber Pinball version is flagged in the room, the invitation and the strip ("no automatic start or live score, needs updating"), instead of looking buggy. The results give each player's rank in the game and their score, no longer "place 1 of the table".
- Game with friends, on original tables (Game of Thrones, GOTG, The Goonies, The Matrix): the game starts by itself (a credit, then Start), the coin door and Start are then ignored; a strip shows everyone's score and ball live; once the game is over, VPX closes by itself and the results open with everyone's score, even without initials entered.
- Game with friends: the others' scores no longer land on the DMD at random, only when it shows the table's score screen (never during an animation, a mode or initials entry). New setting, System menu > Friends > "Friends' scores during the game": automatic, on the DMD, the backglass, the topper, at the bottom or on the side of the playfield; a choice impossible on the cabinet is greyed with its reason. With a PuP-Pack, the strip stays above its windows.
- Game with friends: the other players' strip no longer hides the ball. It leaves the top of the playfield: the scores show on the DMD from time to time (at the start, at each ball, when someone else takes the lead), and stay on the backglass or the topper when the table does not use it. Without these screens, a small inset at the bottom left of the playfield, on the apron.
- Game with friends, on Williams WPC ROM tables (Terminator 2): the same automatic start, live strip and end of game as on original tables, read from the ROM's memory; while entering initials, VPX stays open and Start confirms the letters.
- Game with friends, on Stern SAM ROM tables (Star Trek, The Walking Dead): the same automatic start, live strip and end of game. Player, ball and game over, which their settings memory does not hold, are read through VPX's inspector, enabled for the game with friends only and read on the cabinet itself; a Windows Firewall rule closes VPX to the network, added by the installer along with the cabinet network's rule. On a cabinet already installed: System menu > Friends > Partage et réseau > "Autoriser dans le pare-feu de Windows" (Allow in Windows Firewall), a single Windows request (and again when VPX changes version). As long as the rule is missing, the "État en direct des tables Stern SAM" (Live state of Stern SAM tables) line says so, and these tables play as before. To be confirmed on a real cabinet: the memory addresses of these two games come from their public map.
- The Walking Dead: its balls per game are read, for the ranking as well as for the strip.
- A table that loads another revision of its ROM (an alias, like Star Trek) now gets the group's records, the right ball count and the host's settings in a game with friends: its memory is looked up under the name of the loaded ROM.
- A game that ran counts as played even if VPX closes badly ("The game did not start on their side" no longer shows by mistake).
- Text longer than the screen can be read with the buttons: the release notes of an update, a message… Left flipper on the first action to enter the text (or MagnaSave), right flipper or Start to go down one page, and at the bottom of the text the first action; Exit goes back to the actions. The text frame shows how much has been read. In the VPX settings, MagnaSave scrolls the details of the chosen setting.
- An application log, `data\logs\journal.txt`: everything it does is written down, dated, to understand afterwards what happened on a cabinet.
- Cinema launch: the game logo now lands whole and centered on the backglass, and leaves the playfield whole; a large wheel (medallion) used to spill out the bottom, cut off. A logo taller than it is wide also stays in its slot on the menu wheel.
- "Play with a friend" on a table's page, right under "Play": choose the friend to invite to a live game. An invitation that cannot be sent is no longer hidden: it stays visible, greyed out with its reason ("Offline", "No friend online right now").
- An invitation now arrives even during a game: a chime and a discreet card in the playfield corner (who sends it, the table, the time left), without getting in the way. Leave your game to join your friend, the invitation opens back in the menu; or keep playing, it fades away by itself.
- A table fetched from a friend is recognised in their invitations, even when your cabinet named it differently ("Game of Thrones (Limited Edition)" here, "Game of Thrones LE" on theirs): the same `.vpx` file is the same table. It now keeps, when installed, the name it has on the friend's cabinet.
- Live game: everyone plays the same version of the table, mandatorily, for a score to compete on: the same `.vpx` file and the same script. Without it, no "Accept": "Fetch …'s version" opens their table in their shared library, ticked.
- Live game: the number of balls per game counts. Set differently from the host (5 against 3), you still play, but your score stays out of the ranking; the invitation says so before you accept. It is read from the ROM (ROM tables described by the Pinball Memory Maps project), the script or the table's service menu.
- Live game: your usual table is never modified. The game is played on a copy of the table, made just before launch and deleted on return: the ROM memory, your high scores and your settings stay as they are.
- Live game: everyone plays with the host's settings (balls per game, ball save, tilt…), sent to each friend who accepts and applied to their copy of the table. To play with 5 balls, set your table to 5 before inviting. A friend who did not receive them plays out of the ranking.
- Live game: the group's high score board, per table version. The room gives the record to beat, the results the top five, and in the game the machine shows the group's records instead of the host's. ROM tables too (Terminator 2, Star Trek, The Walking Dead…): their high score table is read and written in the ROM memory of the copy, according to its memory map, checksums included.

## 0.1.4

- Friends (System menu > Friends), between cabinets and with no central server: your friends online with the table they are playing and their latest high scores, finding a player by name or on the local network, friend requests, first badges. Sharing is on by default and is turned off in "Sharing and network", which also says what other cabinets see; Windows asks once to allow Cyber Pinball through its firewall.
- Friends' library, shared by default between friends (turned off in "Sharing and network"): "See their library" on a friend's page shows their tables and the media you are missing. You can fetch a whole table you don't have, or pick part by part those of a table you already have, their version and yours side by side; ROM and PuP-Pack are unticked by default, with a note on rights. Everything goes through the add plan before it is installed; saves, high scores and programs are never sent.
- Live game between friends, up to 4 cabinets: the invitation opens on the friend's cabinet on the same table (version checked), a 5-second countdown starts the game everywhere, then everyone sees the results.
- Friends online at a glance on the tables menu, and small, discreet notifications, one at a time: a friend coming online, a friend request, a finished download. Nothing shows during a game: a summary waits for you when you come back. Can be turned off in "Médias, son et attente" (Media, sound and attract mode).
- A friend's name always follows their latest rename, and a pending friend request is sent again when the cabinets meet.
- Downloads, in the system menu and as a pill on the tables menu: follows everything coming from sites and your friends, resumes by itself where it stopped if the connection drops, even the next day, and takes you back in one move to the screen you asked from.
- Game parts: every file received goes to the part its content calls for (table, PuP-Pack, backglass, ROM, colours, sounds, music…), even when something else was expected; a file to fix no longer blocks installing the rest. A table's sheet has a "Game parts" section to add, replace, reassign or remove each part.
- Downloads: a file can be added to a table, to another game, or installed as a new table.
- Every file to choose (media, backglass, table, PuP-Pack…) is shown as a gallery: preview, download count and rating read from the site, author, version and date, with a sort by popularity; the media saved is always the one you picked.
- "Search the web…" on every add or choice screen: the built-in browser opens on a search already filled in. A part missing from a game starts a search on VPS, Downloads, VPForums and VPUniverse right away.
- More reliable VPUniverse search: short words in the title ("say", "die") no longer empty the search, and the wait imposed by the site is no longer taken for "no results".
- "Jouer et enregistrer" (Play and record), on a table's page: Cyber Pinball films the game, from the moment you chose until the length you chose or until you leave the table, with a small witness in the corner of the playfield. Back in the menu, the video can become the table's presentation on the wheel, after confirmation, or be kept aside.
- System menu in five sections, in this order: Friends, Tables, Media, then Downloads, Cabinet and Cyber Pinball; what is only used when coming from another frontend sits in "Migration".
- Live menu DMD: Cyber Pinball draws the display itself, with the title, PRESS START, credits, the clock and high scores.
- Cinematic launch: on Start, the game logo flies from the playfield to the backglass, the DMD shows loading, a coil thumps when VPX takes over; VPX never waits, and "Media, sound and attract mode" turns it off.
- Interface sounds: a solenoid click when changing table, a coin on Start, the knocker when a high score falls; can be turned off in "Media, sound and attract mode".
- Backglass and DMD in the brand's colours, with a dot font drawn for Cyber Pinball; the DMD colour is adjustable (signal green by default, presets or a free colour).
- The table wheel has sound right after installation: each game's music plays by default, and a table without music plays its playfield video (40%). Cabinets already set up keep their choice: turn "Game music" back on in System menu > Media > "Media, sound and attract mode".
- All sound goes through the cabinet speakers: the music and sounds offered in "Game media", and videos watched in "Get" (YouTube…), no longer go to the Windows default output, often silent on a PinCab.
- Menu lists: a list too long for the screen is marked by a rule at the top or bottom instead of a fade, the selected entry stays whole and every list starts just below its title.
- Long lists: in a content list (games, media, files…), marked by its frame and the "Exit · Leave the list" cue, Exit takes you back to the screen's actions instead of leaving it; MagnaSave (F6 on the keyboard) switches between the two and keeps your place, and holding a flipper scrolls letter by letter.
- Lists no longer move during a download: only a user gesture scrolls them.
- The names under the small medallions of the wheel are larger and can be read from a distance, like every text of the menu.
- Tables menu on a desktop, on a screen narrower than 16:9 (5:4, 4:3): the playfield and the rest of the menu no longer overlap.
- "Récupérer" (Fetch) window: its banner follows the size of the screen, and its texts read as well in 4K as on a small screen; in portrait, its buttons move below.
- Cabinet health (Cabinet section): disk space, tables that don't start, missing media, screens and VPX version on a single screen.
- Dedicated cabinet (Cabinet section), off by default: Cyber Pinball can replace the Windows Explorer, with a way to bring it back.
- Shut down, restart and sleep from the Cyber Pinball section, right after "Quit Cyber Pinball": one confirmation, then settings are saved and every screen turns black, never showing the desktop; on wake, the menu comes back as it was.
- Guest mode (Cabinet section): for a party or the kids, guests can only choose a table, play and read its game page; holding both flippers and both MagnaSaves for 3 seconds unlocks it.
- Cabinet schedule ("Media, sound and attract mode", off by default): like in an arcade, the cabinet dims its screens and goes quiet at night, then sleeps or shuts down at the chosen time if nobody is playing, after a 30-second warning that any button cancels.
- Accelerometer nudge strength, adjustable in the "Tilt" step of the cabinet setup: ×2 is proposed for a Pinscape, which VPX almost ignored at the original scale.
- Windows desktop given back the right way up: Cyber Pinball no longer rotates the playfield screen in the Windows setting, only while it is open; every exit, even after a crash, gives the desktop its previous orientation back. A cabinet already left rotated offers it on first start.
- Table orientation kept from one start to the next: after a restart or a reconnection, tables no longer show upside down in game. "Cabinet health" says whether they face you, and repairs those that were turned in the VPX menu.
- The Windows taskbar no longer shows over the DMD, backglass or playfield when you come back from a game: Cyber Pinball puts it straight back behind, and "Cabinet health" tells you if it had to.
- VPX windows in their place in game: when two screens share the same name, the backglass, the DMD and the score view no longer pile up; Cyber Pinball moves each one back to the screen of its role.
- Game buttons: keyboard keys can be captured again.
- PuP-Packs visible in game: when a table's script keeps its PuP-Pack or PuP-DMD switched off, Cyber Pinball switches it on at install and before each game.
- PuP-Packs the table does not start itself (Game of Thrones LE): Cyber Pinball stores them under the right name and starts them before each game; the doctor says which of the pack's videos cannot trigger with the table's DMD.
- PuP-Pack videos triggered by DMD frames on FlexDMD tables without ROM (Game of Thrones LE): Cyber Pinball recognises these frames during the game and starts the videos, like PinUP Player.
- PuP-Packs: the backglass no longer flickers when the same background video is requested several times in a row (Game of Thrones LE, house selection screen).
- PuP-Packs: images shipped under a video name (Game of Thrones LE) finally show as images, with no flicker, and files with accented names are found; Cyber Pinball fixes this for every pack, on install and before each game.

## 0.1.3

- Buttons of a Pinscape (KL25Z) or of an encoder in gamepad mode: captured in the "Buttons" step and followed by the whole menu and by VPX.
- Optional Pause button in the "Buttons" step: one press pauses the game, another resumes it (P on the keyboard by default).
- Analog plunger (a Pinscape sensor plunger): detected by pulling it, set and tested in the "Plunger" step, along with the accelerometer for nudging.
- Game buttons and interface buttons set separately; keyboard keys and joystick buttons mix freely.
- "Add a table" brings every way of adding a table together in one place, with filters in the catalogue and quick install.
- "What's missing to play": a guided path, from a missing ROM or backglass all the way to "Play".
- A ROM stored in a subfolder of its zip is recognised; "Diagnose and fix" gathers a ROM scattered in a table.
- Correctable waiting list: reassign, empty, replace or remove a file received by mistake; a table's page has a "Files" section.
- An icon for each menu.
- Table menu on a cabinet: help bar reduced to Flippers, Start and Exit ("Cabinet controls" in the system menu), glow of the arrows and the name no longer cut off.
- Tables show the right way up during a game on a cabinet whose flippers are at the bottom.
- Only the table file and its ROM are required to play; DMD content, backglass and PuP-Pack are offered as extras.
- "Downloads" screen: files already received, to reuse or delete; a built-in file picker replaces the Windows one.
