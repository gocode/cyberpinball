# What's new in Cyber Pinball

One section per release, `## <version>` then one `- ` line per item, translating the same
section of [CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) point by point (same versions, same number of items).
`npm run publier` puts both languages in the update feed, where the cabinet shows the notes in
its own language before installing, and in the GitHub release. Short sentences, written for the cabinet's user.

## 0.2.4

- Future Pinball: before downloading, Cyber Pinball checks that 300 MB are free on its disk and says so clearly if not. If extraction fails on your PC (antivirus, permissions, disk), an "Install by hand" screen guides you: open the author's installer, already downloaded and verified, read and accept its agreement, then "I'm done: check" finds Future Pinball wherever you installed it.
- Search the System menu: type a letter (on-screen keyboard, real keyboard or phone), and the matching settings, sections and keywords show up with each letter; a result opens its screen with the cursor on the setting. Also in Help and info › Search.
- The back button of a screen opened after typing on the on-screen keyboard leads to the screen before the typing, no longer to the keyboard.
- A table's point of view: "Find a POV online…" next to "Import a POV…". It opens the same search as the other game parts: the POVs the table database knows, those on VPUniverse and VPForums, those already in Downloads, and "Search the web…" for the rest. The POV you pick is filed with the table. A source that has nothing for this game says so ("VPForums: nothing found for Point of view (POV)") instead of leaving you to wonder whether it was asked. Picking a file from your hard drive is still offered.
- Future Pinball: installing it from Cyber Pinball did not work (the author's installer stops at its licence page, even in silent mode). It is no longer run: Cyber Pinball downloads the file from the author of BAM, checks its fingerprint, shows you both licences in full (Future Pinball's and BAM's) and only extracts the files once you accept, your acceptance being kept next to the game. Extraction uses innoextract, a small free tool also downloaded on demand, with no administrator prompt; files are placed unmodified, and the tables, libraries, music and scripts you already have are never overwritten.
- Future Pinball: this installation has no uninstaller, no shortcuts and no .fpt file association: Cyber Pinball launches the tables itself, and deleting the Future Pinball folder inside Cyber Pinball removes everything. The prompt screen says so before the download.
- Choosing a table's framing: while VPX prepares the preview, the thumbnail shows "VPX is rendering…" with an animated loader, instead of a plain "Rendered by VPX…" that looked like a title. The other "Preview on its way…" tiles (game images, media proposals) get the same loader.
- A table's point of view: the view you set in the VPX menu is kept when a style, an imported POV or the original framing replaces it, and stays offered as "My custom view" so you can go back to it without losing it.
- Table framing: the three formats are renamed to say what they do: "Full frame, bottom cropped", "Whole table, side bands" and "Full frame, table widened".
- A table's point of view (beta): a "Full frame, corners as close as possible" trial brings the playfield corners closer to the screen corners (stretch up to 50% and a raised eye). It is offered for a single table only, never for the cabinet, and the result depends on the table: compare the previews. The raised eye is removed as soon as another framing takes the table back.
- Debug mode: "Windows desktop" (Ctrl+Shift+D) works again while debug mode is on; its window steps aside on the desktop instead of immediately bringing the front-end back.
- Future Pinball: the playfield and the backglass go back to the right cabinet screens. The game remembers the Windows name of a screen ("\\.\DISPLAY2"), which Windows sometimes renumbers: the playfield could open on the DMD screen, screens were rearranged (backglass briefly skewed) and the playfield kept a still image during the game. Cyber Pinball now sets these two screens from your cabinet before every game.
- Future Pinball: Cyber Pinball can now download and install it for you, together with BAM, in its own folder. System menu > Tables > Add a table > "Future Pinball tables" > "Install Future Pinball and BAM…": the screen tells what is downloaded, from where and to where, checks the file's fingerprint, then installs it without a window (Windows asks for your permission). Nothing ships with Cyber Pinball: the authors say nothing about redistribution, so you download from them, with your approval.
- Future Pinball: if the game is already installed elsewhere (C:\vPinball\FuturePinball, C:\Games…), it is still detected and used as is, and nothing is moved. The "Future Pinball tables" screen and adding a table tell which folder is used and why. You can also take over the tables, libraries, music and scripts of the old installation into the Cyber Pinball one: the list, size and destination are shown before the copy, nothing is overwritten, the old installation stays untouched.
- With debug mode on, Cyber Pinball no longer stops at startup on "A JavaScript error occurred in the main process": its first measurement waits for startup to finish, and a failed measurement is logged without interrupting the front-end.
- Challenges: the Arena tab finally has its "Lancer un défi" (Start a challenge) button: it opens the choice of the table, then of the player, instead of explaining a path through the table sheets; with no game played yet it is greyed out with its reason.
- Sharing off: "Régler le partage" (Set up sharing) opens on the spot from the Challenges tab, the Community and my profile, no long path through the settings to follow.
- Players to discover: each suggestion offers "Voir le profil" (View profile) before "Ajouter en ami" (Add as friend); the profile button is also called "Ajouter en ami".
- My profile tells the truth about its state: "Partage désactivé" (Sharing off), "Profil public désactivé" (Public profile off) or "Profil public en cours de préparation" (Public profile being prepared), three situations that are no longer mixed up.
- Profile of a player whose activity is hidden: the header says so, the Badges tab no longer shows a false "Aucun encore" (None yet), and Start on the card shows the activity again in one gesture.
- A level too high for the known seniority shows "Niveau en cours de vérification" (Level being checked) instead of "Niveau ?".
- Start on another player's post announces "Tu aimes cette publication" (You like this post) or "Tu n'aimes plus cette publication" (You no longer like this post), and the title note says what Start does.
- The daily tables opened from the Arena offer "Retour : Arène" (Back: Arena) rather than "Retour : Classements".
- "À proximité" (Nearby) becomes "Sur mon réseau local" (On my local network) in the player filter and in the "Find a player" section.
- "Instantanée" (Instant) becomes "Parties en direct" (Live games) everywhere (menus, sheets, notes); the generic labels of a game between friends are now called "Partie entre amis" (Game between friends), so they are no longer mixed up with a live game.
- The whole interface now addresses the player informally in French: no more formal "vous", "votre", "vos", "veuillez" or plural imperatives in screens, phone page and messages; a test guards the rule.
- Table framing and Point of view: the framing pictures now show. VPX rendered them, but the front-end refused to display them (empty cards).
- Fixed: when playing alone at a table, leaving the table in the middle of a game no longer passes what the ROM had at the moment of the stop off as a final score. The score is only read when VPX closes (the ROM only writes its memory then): if it says the game was not over, the score is kept on your cabinet, marked "game left" in the table's score board, but it is not published to the rankings, not counted in a challenge and not kept as a level record.
- A game played to the end, or whose score enters the table's high score list, counts as before. When the ROM's map does not say whether the game is over (some machines), the game also counts as before.
- New: debug mode (System menu > My cabinet > Health and backups > Debug mode). It shows live, in a corner of the screen of your choice (the backglass by default, otherwise the DMD, otherwise the playfield), the measurements of all your screens: frame rate of each front-end window, VPX frame rate during a game over the last minute, stalls and their likely cause, media loading times and launch steps, processor, memory, graphics card. A compact line or a detailed panel, and on the DMD a bar readable from a distance.
- Debug mode stays out of the way of the game: the window is transparent, never takes focus or clicks, does not show in recordings and never opens in the headset; on the playfield the panel is reduced so it does not hide the table. When it is off, nothing runs.
- The last 10 minutes of measurements can go with the technical report (debogage.json file, with no path or account name): "Attach to reports" on the same screen.
- The VPX DMD plugin can report its frame rate every second; it only does so when Cyber Pinball asks for it, from the next build of the plugin.
- Patched VPX "patched Cyber Pinball 6": on a cabinet with DudesCab boards, VPX no longer crashes while closing (0xC0000005) when DOF is unloaded. The VPX 10.8.1 DOF library shut the board down a second time after it was really closed; Cyber Pinball now installs before every game a DOF library (`plugins/dof/dof64.dll`) rebuilt with the fixes its authors have published since. The VPX DOF plugin and your configuration do not change.
- If VPX still crashes this way while closing, the failure screen says so clearly: the game was played, nothing needs fixing in the table, it is a known flaw of the VPX 10.8.1 DOF library, fixed from patch set 6 of Cyber Pinball's VPX on.
- Table framing (beta): System menu › My cabinet › VPX › Table framing. On a cabinet, a table cannot fill a 16:9 screen without a compromise: you choose between "Full screen, apron cropped" (default), "Whole table" and "Full screen, table widened", each shown on your own table, rendered by VPX without anything appearing on the cabinet.
- Point of view of a table: each framing is shown there on a picture of the table, another style can be chosen for that table alone, and "Restore the previous framing" goes back. A table taken over before this version gets back the framing of its original installation if that installation is still on the disk.
- A table's journey offers "Choose the framing" and, when the VPS database knows one, a community POV.
- In game, the MagnaSave settings have a "Framing of this table" line, which opens the "Point of View" page of the VPX menu: framing is adjusted there live and saved for the table.
- Profile, Community and Arena: in the tab bar, Exit no longer leaves the screen at once. It hands you back the content of the tab on screen, and a second Exit leaves the screen. From the content, the flippers reach the tab bar in its place, below the header.
- In the tab bar, Tab and Shift+Tab on the keyboard switch tabs like the flippers: the lit tab is always the one you see, never a tab you passed over without its content.
- Virtual reality enabled but headset unplugged or taken off: Cyber Pinball no longer looks for the headset during a game on the screens. Those attempts, every 15 seconds, made VPX drop frames on every table (about 115 fps instead of 120, with regular stutters).
- Tables taken over from another installation (PinUP Popper, PinballY, a VPX folder) keep exactly the framing they had. Cyber Pinball reads the settings of the VPX that played them (its VPinballX.ini, or the registry for a VPX older than 10.8) and freezes for each one its mode (Manual, Fit Table, Fit Screen), the screen measurements and the view it read, without ever touching the original installation. A POV lying next to the table, which the old VPX ignored, is no longer applied on its own. Table page > Manage the table… > Point of view shows it ("Framing from the original installation") and lets you switch to the cabinet framing.
- A temporary file that is still open no longer blocks cancelling or completing a table import. Cleanup retries in the background; if it still fails, temporary files are kept and navigation continues.
- Colour sampling no longer accumulates videos from tables that have been left: only one sample stays active and replaced subjects are discarded. Temporarily unreadable media wait before being retried.
- Hidden video sampling players release their decoder after success, failure or timeout. An unresponsive frame seek is stopped, and a conversion page whose renderer crashed is recreated on its next use.
- After the graphics decoding service disconnects, the front-end retries the video once. This incident no longer marks the file as damaged in diagnostics; previous errors of this type are ignored.
- The phone remote removes its listeners when leaving the built-in browser: successive openings no longer accumulate loading notifications.
- Patched VPX, patch set 5: automatically importing a legacy POV view file no longer crashes when launching a table directly. Empty or invalid legacy numeric fields are ignored without aborting loading; valid view and quality settings are preserved.
- Pre-merge check: a test is stopped when it has made no progress for 5 minutes, no longer when its test file runs longer than 5 minutes; long but healthy files are no longer wrongly interrupted.
- A green check is still recognized after merging the shared branch in when only the line numbers of the work have shifted.
- The tech sheet lists all graphics cards in its summary and names the card concerned by each driver warning, without assuming which one the game uses.
- The diagnosis shows companion scripts that VPX could not load before subsequent variable or object errors, only for the last game of the relevant table.
- Analog gamepad triggers can be assigned to front-end and game buttons, with their SDL axis and direction passed through to VPX. Cabinet analog sensors are not treated as buttons.
- On a single display, the wizard continues to the embedded playfield DMD option even when no display is available for the backglass.
- VPX 10.8.0: launch without the editor window and exit with the cabinet’s Quit button even when this VPX uses different button mappings.
- VPX 10.8.0: use the supplied POV when partial view settings hid it, in a separate settings file; an existing custom view keeps priority. Diagnostics read the log from this version’s folder.
- Diagnostics: small, nearly empty volumes are no longer reported as almost full; EDID refresh rate margins no longer trigger advice to change cables.
- Legacy .NET ActiveX components used by table scripts now use the .NET runtime available on Windows, even without .NET 3.5. This prevents music initialization failures followed by cascading UltraDMD errors, without changing tables.
- The diagnosis shows the first script error and the number of errors from the table’s last game. It keeps different tables separate and clears the finding after a new game without errors.

## 0.2.3

- What’s new: eight highlights for 0.2.3, including each table’s point of view and Cyber Pinball’s patched VPX, with previews and direct links.
- Profile Badges tab: full catalogue of earned and unearned badges, with XP rewards for each; my profile shows current progress and the remaining target.
- Builds reuse VPX and its PuP plugin when their hashes and those of the sources, patches, dependencies and build tools match the last successful build.
- Profile: all information remains reachable with the flippers, even without an associated action. My activity also opens an empty likes list; current tables can be found on the wheel when only their title is shared.
- Profile: the tab bar receives focus on arrival, as in Arena and Community. Flippers display each section immediately; Start enters its content and Exit returns to the tabs.
- Community: online players appear first in Players; the discovery strip has a clearer heading.
- Arena: score rankings come first, with country and worldwide position badges on recently played tables.
- VPX: the active preset has a checkmark and a contrasting badge.
- In System menu > My cabinet > VPX > Presets, choose Auto, Performance, Balanced, Quality, Ultra or Custom. Existing cabinets keep their current settings in Custom after updating; no preset applies without your choice.
- Auto starts conservatively from the graphics card and playfield, then adjusts quality between games using measured GPU rendering times. VR profiles, table-specific settings and cabinet configurations remain independent.
- Custom is always available and restores the settings saved before choosing a preset. Manually changing a graphics setting also disables automatic adaptation.
- VPX 10.8.1-5436 patched Cyber Pinball 4 records GPU rendering times in its log for this adaptation; previous fixes remain included.
- Arena opens with the tab bar selected, so the flippers switch tabs immediately. In Arena and Community, the entire tab responds to clicks, even during a refresh.
- A player's profile is redesigned like a social network profile, in the spirit of the tabbed Community: at the top, their large avatar (ring of their level, green ring when online), their flag, name and level, what they are doing right now and their numbers (friends, records, badges, groups, tables in common).
- The actions of your relationship sit at the top of the profile, the main one first and selected on opening: Invite to play, Write and Their library for a friend; Add (+25 XP), Accept or Decline, Cancel the request for another player; Welcome them for a newcomer. An unavailable action stays visible, greyed out, with its reason ("Offline", "Friends only").
- Four tabs, MagnaSave switches between them: Activity (their records, badges and games, each with its thumb; their tables of the week; their level and rarest badges), Records (their published scores, your tables first, with what you still have to catch up; Start puts the wheel on the table), Badges (all of theirs, by rarity) and About (country, friendship, library, identifier, invited players, public groups, then Hide their activity, Remove from my friends and Block).
- My profile is the same screen: what other players see, with Edit my profile, Privacy and My account at the top; Start on one of my posts shows who liked it.
- An interrupted prerelease can be resumed through grouped publishing without changing its version number.
- Community and Arena: the top tabs remain clickable with the mouse from every navigation state, without having to press Exit.
- Player challenges: challenge a friend or a ranked player on the exact version of a table, with your best score or the one from the game you just played; they have 48 h, unlimited tries, and both of you can still raise your score. At the deadline, the higher score wins 40 XP, then "Rematch" in one press. The challenged player can decline.
- Challenges travel without a server, from cabinet to cabinet, signed by each player: they arrive even if your two cabinets are never on at the same time. Only scores read by Cyber Pinball count; a blocked player, or one banned from the rooms, cannot challenge you.
- In the Arena, the "Challenges" tab opens (your turn, sent, recent results), the "Challenge from …" card shows up in "Play now", and the dot-matrix board of the poster announces a challenge about to expire. "Challenge" now works on "Your place is taken" and on the game return screen, and a table sheet offers "Play together… › Challenge a player…".
- Notifications: challenge received, "Nina beat your score: your turn", 6 h left, result, declined; a single line per challenge in Recent activity. A table version you do not have stays visible, greyed out, with "Get this version" from a friend who has it. None of this in local play.
- Community: suggested players and friend requests show the tables you have in common as overlapping medallions (your favourites first), then "+2 tables in common" when there are more.
- Community: the Players tab shows who is online right now, and "Everyone online" opens the full list.
- Community: friends in common show on suggested players and on received friend requests as overlapping avatars, and bring those players to the top of the suggestions. You choose who sees your friends (My profile › Privacy › What others see): public (the default), your friends only, or private.
- Arena: a Scores tab leads straight to the scoreboards. It lists the tables you played recently, with the leader of each and your place; Start opens a table's scoreboard. Below come all tables (search, scope, period) and the player ranking.
- Arena: outside Home, each tab reads as a list. The flippers move from one line to the next and Start confirms it, with no block to enter; Home keeps its blocks for its summaries and shortcuts.
- Arena and Community: in a tab, Exit puts the cursor back on the tab bar, on the open tab; a second Exit returns to the wheel. The help bar says so.
- Arena: the Challenges tab gets the crossed flippers icon, and the trophy moves to Scores.
- 3D glasses: the wheel is in 3D too. Its medallions come out of the screen (the selected one in front of its neighbours), along with the band, the title plate, the counter, the help bar, the logo and the gear button; the table background and, on a desktop, the chrome frames stay flat. "Wheel in 3D" setting in System menu > My cabinet > Virtual reality and 3D vision > 3D glasses, on by default with the glasses.
- Point of view (beta): a table's sheet, under "Manage the table…", says what frames it and lets you choose: the cabinet framing (the same for every table), the POV supplied with the table, or "Import a POV…" (a .pov, or the .ini of a POV for VPX 10.8, from Downloads, a USB stick or a drive). The imported POV is stored with the table and applied to it only; a POV that would turn it the wrong way on the cabinet is refused, with the reason.
- Arena: the world player ranking is on Home, right under the card of the moment. It shows the top three and your place, and Start opens the full ranking (Exit returns to the Arena). Your world and country places also open their ranking directly.
- Levels: a game you open that others join brings 25 XP, once per group of players. Playing again with the same people brings nothing more; a different group (one more player, one less, or others) brings it again.
- Arena and Community: the entries at the bottom of the sections (open a game, all games, all rankings, my badges, see everything…) look like the other menus, as action lines with no box around them; the flippers reach them and Start confirms.
- Patched VPX "patched Cyber Pinball 3": the PuP plugin no longer freezes the game when a table shows a large animated image (full screen GIF of the PuP-Pack). It decoded the image while blocking rendering, 200 to 300 ms each time (stutters in The Matrix); decoding now runs alongside, without stopping the game.
- Table framing (beta): on a 16:9 screen, a table is 17 to 29% too narrow, and VPX's "Fit table" framing filled the width with a zoom that could cut off the back of the playfield (on Attack from Mars, the saucer left the screen). With Cyber Pinball's patched VPX (patched Cyber Pinball 2), the table is enlarged as long as the back of the playfield stays on screen: the decorative bottom, below the flippers, and the empty space above the table are what leave the screen, and the rest of the width is filled by a slight stretch (15% at most). "Keep the top of the table" and "Maximum width stretch" settings in VPX settings > Table view and fitting, also in the VPX menu in game, with eye height and distance for the perspective.
- POV files (beta): the point of view prepared by the community for a table (.pov, or "<table> POV.ini") is now applied to that table only, if the flippers stay on your side of the cabinet; a POV made for another screen is set aside. When a framing was already chosen for the table, the POV is offered in the diagnosis.
- Diagnosis: a table switched to manual view in the VPX menu without a view set is no longer shown as "Framing set for this table"; a repair gives it back the cabinet's framing.
- Community: the screen opens with the cursor in the tab bar; the flippers move from tab to tab and show its content right away, Start enters it, Exit goes back up to the bar.
- The Community home sums up each tab (Messages, Players, Groups, Rooms, Profile): Start on a summary opens the tab on the same strip, Exit brings you back.
- Online players are shown in wider cards, two per row: the nickname fits on one line, "Add" no longer moves when the cursor passes, and "All players" opens the full list.
- 3D glasses (beta): tables are played in 3D with simple colour filter glasses (red / cyan, green / magenta or amber / blue), in System menu > My cabinet > Virtual reality and 3D vision > 3D glasses. They combine with 3D vision: the depth then follows your head too.
- Glasses calibration with the cabinet buttons, in front of the playfield screen: six settings, one eye closed each time, for less ghosting and truer colours. Depth and colours are set in the same place.
- The 3D effect goes through a copy of the table's settings, given to VPX for the game: neither the cabinet's VPinballX.ini nor the table's own file is changed. SMAA anti-aliasing, which VPX does not apply in 3D, is replaced by quality FXAA for the game.
- What's new: the System menu entry now counts its nine sections, the Arena included.
- What's new: every entry now has its picture, a screen of the feature or an illustration, in English and French (twelve had none: the Arena, today's tables, conversations with the team, the System menu, sound, Future Pinball, Pinball FX, importing without duplicating, other VPX versions, DOF, credits, 3D vision).
- Install plan from another installation: it now says large files are linked and the others copied, instead of saying everything is copied.
- English: texts that mention a screen or a button use its English name (backups, DOF, friends, DMD, headset controllers), instead of the French one.
- Place taken: when a player passes you in today's (or the all-time) ranking on a table version you have played, a notification says so ("Léa passed you on Attack from Mars"), at most once per table and per day, and stays in Recent activity; "Win back my place" starts the table directly.
- Arena: the place just taken from you becomes the top card of the home tab ("Win back my place", "Write to them" between friends, "Challenge" greyed out until challenges exist) and the first page of the poster's board.
- Game return: after a game that brings something new (record beaten, player passed), a screen shows the score and the record, the XP earned on the level bar with their breakdown, the player passed, the place gained and the next one to beat, then "Play again", "A word to …" (ready-made phrases, between friends) and "Back to the tables"; the badge and level animation comes next.
- The Community moves to the Arena's tabbed screen: Home, Messages, Players, Groups, Rooms and Profile, with the same block navigation (flippers move from block to block, Start enters, Exit leaves, MagnaSave switches tabs).
- The Community home leads with what is waiting for you: the conversation where someone wrote to you, with the last message's bubble and three ready-made replies sent with one press.
- "Online" shows your friends with their presence ring and what they are doing; when few people are around, players close to you (tables in common, same country, close level, newcomers) are suggested, to add with one press (+25 XP per new friend).
- Your country's room can be read live on the home tab, with its ready-made phrases; "What's happening" offers to welcome a newcomer or congratulate a friend's feat.
- Each player shows their level ("Lvl 18") and their tier ring.
- "Introduce Cyber Pinball": a QR code to the download page, on the home tab on a desktop and in the Players tab on a cabinet.
- New Community medallion poster: the message waiting for you, your friends online, your country's room scrolling by, your groups and friend requests. Level and rankings are now on the Arena poster.
- "My friends" and "Online players" are now a single entry, "Players" (System menu > Community > Players): the Friends tab comes first, with received requests at the top.
- After the update that brings it, the patched VPX is installed as soon as Cyber Pinball starts: the technical sheet shows "patched Cyber Pinball 1" right away, and the cabinet health no longer keeps the backglass refresh rate warning.
- Table search: clearing the "Search for a title" field and confirming now clears the search, as stated; before, it stayed until Cyber Pinball was restarted. "Clear the filters" also removes the searched title.
- All text fields: a field cleared and confirmed is now taken into account (room topic removed, ZeDMD Wi-Fi turned off, Art-Net address on the whole network, Juke-box correction back to the original information, Rankings search cleared). Exit still cancels.
- New "Arena" medallion on the wheel, between Community and Collections: everything played against others. Its live medallion shows a cup and confetti; hide it in System menu › Tables › Wheel ("Arena on the wheel").
- When focused, the Arena poster shows your level and what one game can still bring, then a records board in DMD dots whose pages turn every six seconds, the most pressing first (open game, place to win back, your ranking, your records), and who is playing right now.
- Start opens the Arena screen: the "Your level" band, icon tabs switched with the MagnaSave buttons, and cards picked with the flippers. On top, the most pressing action (a place just taken from you, an instant game waiting for you), then the games open on your tables and the XP each action brings. The Daily tab opens the tables of the day; the Challenges and League tabs arrive in a coming version.
- System menu: new Arena section, with Instant games, Rankings and Progress (My level, My badges, Game history), which leave Community. "Play locally" also removes the Arena.
- DMD colors (Serum, PIN2DMD): a colorization dropped on its own is stored under the name of the ROM the table plays, with the file names VPX reads (`<rom>.cRZ`, `pin2dmd.pal` and `pin2dmd.vni`). Before, a `pin2dmd.pal` ended up in `vni\pin2dmd` and the DMD stayed uncolored. There is nothing to tick: VPinMAME's F1 window does not exist with Cyber Pinball's VPX, the colors apply by themselves.
- Colors already stored under the wrong name are renamed automatically before the next game. The table diagnostic also reports a colorization made for another revision of the ROM (one click to try it) and files VPX never reads (`.pac`, `.cROM`).
- Stutters in game: the cabinet report now also tells where the VPX render thread was during each freeze (graphics driver, video, texture loading…), not only the game loop, which is then just waiting for that thread.
- A game slowed down by freezes (antivirus, script) is no longer mistaken for a game limited by a screen's refresh rate.
- Tables of the day (Community › Leaderboards): the most played tables today worldwide, in your country or among friends, the record of the day with its author's avatar and level, your place and your gap to the player just ahead of you; Start launches the table. Few games today: the week shows instead, never an empty board. A version other than yours stays visible, greyed, with "Get this version" from a friend who has it.
- Your games of the day and of the week, per exact table version, travel with your signed public card (capped; "?" when they are implausible).
- Table page: "On this table" shows the goal (the player just above you), the record of the day, the friend playing it right now and your friends this week; a table nobody has played online says your next score will be the first. Your record and your group move to the statistics; the files stay in "Manage the table…".
- Table page: an instant game open on your version comes before "Play", which states the goal; "Play together…" groups "Play with a friend" and "Instant game" ("Challenge a player" comes later).
- Launching a table: the "Goal" card on Cyber Pinball's loading screen and in the desktop panel, never over a loading video or image you chose, and with no added sound.
- ROM tables: the first game no longer stops on the "FACTORY SETTINGS RESTORED" screen, where coins made a sound without giving credits and you had to quit and relaunch. Without a memory, a ROM starts like a machine with a dead battery and waits for the coin door button. Cyber Pinball now does this first start-up itself, with no screen and no sound: in the background as soon as a ROM table has no memory yet (a table just installed), and while the game loads if it is still missing. A memory already there is never replaced.
