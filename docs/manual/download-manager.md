## Download manager

One place to follow everything that is coming, from a site or a friend, the way a game console does: **System menu > Downloads** (at the root, third line, after Tables and My cabinet: from the table screen, Exit opens the System menu on the last root entry chosen, and Downloads stays there once chosen).
On the tables menu, a **pill** left of the gear sums it up ("2 en cours · 45 %" (2 downloading · 45%), "En attente" (Waiting), "1 bloqué" (1 blocked), "1 prêt" (1 ready), a single text at a time, by priority; "blocked" and "ready" are news, gone as soon as the list is opened); a click opens it, Exit brings you back.

- **The list**: "En cours et en attente" (Downloading and waiting), then "Terminés et bloqués" (Finished and blocked), in the order they were requested.
  Each row gives its source (a friend, a site, VPinMediaDB, the update), its state as a word ("En cours" (Downloading), "En attente de Bruno" (Waiting for Bruno), "Nouvel essai à 22:31" (Next try at 22:31), "En pause" (Paused), "Prêt" (Ready), "Échec" (Failed)), what is received and what is left, its bar (blue downloading, amber waiting, green ready, red blocked; hatched with no known size), and the next step with its sign: **↩** the screen it takes you back to, **↻** when it resumes, **‖** paused, **!** what to do.
  Rows update in place: a row only changes section, and the list only gains or loses a row, on a gesture (opening, an action), and the cursor follows the download.
  At the bottom: "Tout mettre en pause" (Pause all) (or "Tout reprendre" (Resume all)), "Retirer les terminés" (Clear finished) (never the blocked ones), "Fichiers téléchargés" (Downloaded files) (the inventory, below) and "Retour" (Back).
- **A download's sheet**: its state, what is received (file n of N, rate, time left), its source, its resume, the screen it takes you back to and where it is stored.
  Actions depending on the state: **"Revenir à …" (Go back to …)** (finished: the original screen with what was received; in progress: the original screen, the download carries on), "Mettre en pause" (Pause), "Reprendre" (Resume), "Réessayer maintenant" (Try again now) (a site waiting for its next try), "Annuler le téléchargement" (Cancel the download) (after a confirmation, the cursor on "Garder le téléchargement" (Keep the download): what was received is deleted), "Retirer de la liste" (Remove from the list).
  A friend who is away: "Revenir à …" first, resuming is automatic.
- **The next step of a finished job first**: each kind of download can declare its next step (`DownloadNextStep`, set by `nextStep`).
  A friend's table not installed yet: the state "Reçue, pas encore installée" (Received, not installed yet), the line "→ Reçue, pas encore installée · Start : Installer dans ma bibliothèque", and in its sheet "Installer dans ma bibliothèque" (Install in my library) first, instead of "Revenir à …"; a friend's media not saved yet: "Vérifier et enregistrer les médias" (Check and save the media).
  Once done (the table installed, its received folder put away), the sheet goes back to the usual "Revenir à …".
  A skipped file shows in the sheet, with its reason.
- **Back to the original screen**: each download remembers the screen it was requested from, as data that survives a restart.
  A table's media received from a friend take you back to that table's "Médias du jeu" (Game media), the files in the draft, ready to save; a friend's table, or parts, to the add plan ("Annuler" (Cancel) goes back to "Parties du jeu" (Game parts) or to the friend's library); a waiting-list file to the waiting list or to its table's game parts; a VPinMediaDB media item to "Médias du jeu"; the update to "Mise à jour de Cyber Pinball" (Cyber Pinball update).
  If that screen no longer exists (table removed, friend deleted), a message says so, then the fallback: the library of the friend the download came from, otherwise the manager.
  A download that finishes while its transfer screen is open is handed over at once; otherwise a notification says so on the tables menu ("Téléchargement fini" (Download finished), see "Tables menu notifications"), a banner on a full screen ("Téléchargement terminé : …" (Download finished: …)), and the pill and the system menu entry say it is "ready" (red dot on the entry, its Tables section and the gear).
  These dots are notifications: **opening the list is enough to read them**, without opening each line; the list marks them seen every time it is drawn, even for a download that finishes while you look at it (`seen`, `DownloadSummary.unseen`, `downloadsAttention`).
  What stays ready is then read in the entry's text ("3 prêts · prêt : Juke-box" (3 ready · ready: Jukebox)), without a dot.
- **Never twice**: a download started from a menu carries a key (`dedupe`: "friend-table:<friend>:<table>", "friend-media:<friend>:<table>"); `add` with a key already running, waiting, paused, or finished but not delivered returns that one, adding nothing (`pendingDownload`, the log says "déjà demandé, pas relancé", already asked for, not started again), and the screen that started it shows its state.
  A screen that restarts a download it already follows (`track` of a running id) is flagged in the log.
  In the built-in browser, the same link clicked while it is downloading does not start again: "Déjà en cours : …
  Il continue, rien n'est relancé." (Already in progress: … It carries on, nothing is started again.)
  Test: `packages/core/src/test/reception-ami.test.ts` (no duplicate, "in progress" state visible on the table, parts screen opened in the list, the ROM's path through the declaration, and the key required of every `manager.add` in the application).
- **Resuming**: each file builds up in a partial file kept from one session to the next, and what resumes starts from the size of the partial on disk, never from a counter: nothing is lost, nothing arrives twice.
  A **friend** who goes offline puts the download "En attente de …" (Waiting for …); as soon as they are connected again (checked every three seconds), the cabinet asks them for the file from what it already has, they re-read and hash the beginning then send the rest.
  A **site page** (built-in browser, VPUniverse, VPForums…): Chromium resumes a short cut by itself; a long cut leaves the partial and the download's state (URLs followed, ETag, date), and the manager resumes it at the next try (15 s, 30 s, 1 min, 2 min, 5 min, 10 min, then every 15 min), in the same cookie session, after a restart too.
  A **direct URL** (VPinMediaDB, update, VPX, yt-dlp) asks for the rest (`Range`, `If-Range` header); if the site sends everything, the partial starts over from zero.
  What is followed by its screen (a VPinMediaDB plan, the update, VPX) keeps its partial: relaunched from that screen, it resumes there.
  The queue is written to `<données>\telechargements.json` at every state change (progress at most every two seconds): it survives closing Cyber Pinball and restarting the PC; what was running waits for its source at the next start and resumes by itself.
  A paused download never resumes by itself.
- **Verification**: a resumed file is verified in full, like a file received in one go.
  Between friends, the SHA-256 fingerprint sent at the end covers the whole file, and the receiver hashes the beginning it had before adding the rest; a media item also passes its content check.
  A direct URL: the index MD5 (VPinMediaDB), the manifest SHA-256 (update, VPX, yt-dlp).
  A wrong fingerprint after a resume (the kept beginning was damaged) restarts the file from zero, once; a wrong file is never handed over.
  A cabinet from before resuming sends everything: the partial starts over from zero.
- **Invariants** (tests): every download source in the application is listed and goes through the manager, and only `resumable.ts` writes an HTTP response body to disk (`downloadsources.test.ts`); an interrupted download (site cut, friend going offline in the middle of sending a table, simulated restart) resumes with nothing lost and nothing twice, a damaged beginning is caught at the end, a pause is never relaunched, every return screen is valid or has its fallback (`downloads-resume.test.ts`).
- **Code**: `packages/core/src/downloadqueue.ts` (queue, states, return screen and fallback, texts), `packages/core/src/downloadmanager.ts` (scheduling, resuming, writing the queue) and `packages/core/src/resumable.ts` (HTTP resume); sources in `app/src/download-sources.ts` (friend, direct URL) and `app/src/acquire-window.ts` (`siteTransport`); screen in `app/src/download-center.ts`, pill in `app/renderer/frontend.js`.
