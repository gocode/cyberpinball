## Tables menu notifications

On the tables menu, left of the gear, what happens on the network and in downloads shows without covering anything: the **friends online pill** (the friends icon, a green dot, the number; absent when no friend is online; a click opens "Mes amis" (My friends)) left of the downloads pill, and **one notification at a time**, discreet, that fades away on its own.
On the desktop it sits on the brand's line, left of the pills, and stops before the playfield and backglass frames; on a cabinet the area is narrow, so it moves below the pills, aligned with them on the right, away from the wheel and the title.
It never hides the table's media or the wheel.

- **What is announced**, most urgent first: a friend request received ("Demande d'ami : Zoé" (Friend request: Zoé), amber; a click opens "Demandes d'amis" (Friend requests)), a request accepted ("Zoé a accepté votre demande" (Zoé accepted your request)), a blocked download (the cause first: "Plus de place sur le disque · …" (No space left on the disk · …), red), a finished download ("Téléchargement fini" (Download finished), the table and where it came from, green; a click opens Downloads), a friend coming online ("Bruno vient de se connecter" (Bruno just came online), blue), a friend's record or badge (green), a friend starting a game ("Bruno lance une partie" (Bruno is starting a game), the table, blue).
  The words always say what happens; the colour only repeats it (blue for information, green for success).
- **Never a stack**: a single notification on screen, about 6 seconds, then a pause before the next.
  What arrives together, or while another one is showing, is grouped by kind ("3 amis viennent de se connecter" (3 friends just came online), the names as detail); three kinds or more waiting together, back from a game for instance, make a single summary ("4 nouveautés" (4 new notifications), "1 demande d'ami · 1 téléchargement fini · …"); a click opens [Recent activity](recent-activity.md#recent-activity), which says each one in full.
- **On every screen**: a single rule says, for each Cyber Pinball screen, where the notification area sits, whether the number of friends online shows there and which notifications appear (`noticeZone`, [noticezone.ts](../../packages/core/src/noticezone.ts)); a single place names the current screen (`zoneScreen` in `app/src/frontend.ts`), and the queue, the drawn corner and the line laid over VPX follow it.
  The wheel: the corner, left of the gear.
  The system menu and each of its sub-screens (settings, details pages, lists, wizard, friends…), the controls help, a message, "Quitter Cyber Pinball ?" (Quit Cyber Pinball?), attract mode: the number of friends and every notification, on the gear's line, right of the brand, above the screen's header.
  The built-in browser, when it covers the playfield screen: every notification, as a line laid over its page, bottom left (never on its bar); the number stays on the menu below.
  During a game and its loading: no counter, only what matters (an invitation, a friend coming online, the game with friends), over VPX; nothing at all with "Masquer les notifications pendant les parties" (Hide notifications during games).
  Only the startup opening (a few seconds), the black shutdown screen and the Windows desktop have no area; a short notice under the wheel's brand and the shutdown warning make the notification wait, never the counter.
  Nothing is lost: what waits appears on the next screen that allows it.
  Guest mode changes nothing: what a notification opens goes through the padlock.
  An invitation that arrives opens by itself on the wheel, in the system menu and on the help, over the open screen, the cursor on "Répondre plus tard" (Answer later); "Répondre plus tard" or "Retour" (Back) gives that screen back as it was, cursor included (`library.interrupt`; never during a long operation or typing, where only the notification announces it).
  Test: [notifications-partout.test.ts](../../packages/core/src/test/notifications-partout.test.ts) walks every known screen (`ZONE_SCREENS`), every menu phase, and the real queue screen by screen.
- **Never at the wrong moment**: what a screen does not show (see above) waits for the next screen that allows it, then expires (10 minutes for a connection, 30 for a finished download, one hour for a friend request) or drops if it no longer applies (the friend left again, the request was answered, the download was opened).
  The same event is not repeated before its delay (a friend whose connection comes and goes: once every quarter of an hour).
  At startup, what is already there (friends, requests, finished downloads) is not news, nor are friends connecting in the first ten seconds: it is the cabinet arriving; the pill counts them.
  A friend just accepted is not announced as "just came online", nor are old records (more than a quarter of an hour) a friend sends again when reconnecting.
- **A single mechanism**: a finished download its screen did not take back is announced by the notification on the tables menu; on a full screen, by the usual short banner, and the notification does not repeat it afterwards.
- **Game with friends**: its news comes before everything else, one at a time, 10 seconds, never in a summary.
  An invitation ("Bruno vous invite à rejoindre une partie" (Bruno invites you to join a game), the table; it shows with the chime, without waiting), a guest accepts ("Bruno a accepté votre invitation" (Bruno accepted your invitation), "Pour lancer : Menu système > Communauté > Joueurs" (To start: System menu > Community > Players); together: "Bruno et Léa ont accepté" (Bruno and Léa accepted), or "3 invités ont accepté" (3 guests accepted) when the whole names do not fit; green), declines, with the reason ("David a décliné votre invitation" (David declined your invitation) · "N'a pas cette table" (Does not have this table), "N'a pas la même version" (Does not have the same version), "Occupé pour l'instant" (Busy for now); "Pas de réponse de David" (No answer from David); blue), a player leaves the game or does not start it, the host cancels it (amber), the game is over for everyone while we were already back from ours ("Partie entre amis : Bruno gagne" (Game with friends: Bruno wins), or "vous gagnez !" (you win!), then "Résultats : Menu système > Communauté · vous : place 2 sur 3" (Results: System menu > Community · you: rank 2 of 3); green).
  It shows even with notifications off or "counter only"; never on the game's screen (room, start, results) nor on the invitation's, which already say it, nor what the band of the game in progress already shows (a player leaving, the end).
  A click opens the game's screen; with the buttons, its entry is at the top of System menu > Community > "Joueurs" (Players) (red dot up to the gear).
  Results not yet seen open by themselves back from a game on another table.
  The survey compares the game from one moment to the next, whatever message changed it (`packages/core/src/livenotices.ts`: `liveSnapshot`, `liveNotices`, `liveOutcome`, and `inviteNotices` for invitations); the `live` rules of `NOTICE_RULES` do the rest.
  Tests: `packages/core/src/test/notifications-partie.test.ts`.
- **Everywhere, even in a game**: the game with friends, an invitation and a friend coming online show on the tables menu, **in the system menu** (same place: on the gear's line, right of the brand) and **during games**, over VPX, in the playfield's top-left corner for the player, under an invitation's card if there is one, in the direction VPX really draws the table (see "Game news" in [Friends](friends-the-cabinet-network.md#community-the-cabinet-network)); without focus: VPX keeps control (`NoticeRule.away`, `NoticeContext.away`).
  The others (downloads, records, badges, friend requests) show on every Cyber Pinball screen, not during a game.
  Each has its sound, played as it comes in, once, in the menu as in a game: the **friend signal** when a friend comes online, the **ding** of the game with friends, and for an invitation, the usual chime, when it arrives (see [Interface sounds](game-media.md#interface-sounds)).
  A friend who comes and goes does not ring all the time: a reconnection within the presence tolerance (15 s, `PRESENCE_GRACE_MS`) is not a connection, and the same friend is not announced again, nor rings, for a quarter of an hour.
  **System menu > Community > "Réglages de la communauté" > "Masquer les notifications pendant les parties"** (Hide notifications during games; off by default; Start toggles; kept in `<données>\amis\notifications-en-partie.json`): on, nothing shows or rings during a game, neither line, nor card, nor chime; what matters waits in the menu afterwards (an invitation still valid opens on return, the friends pill counts them, the news waits its delay).
- **Setting**: **Menu système (System menu) > Tables > "Son et mode attente" (Sound and attract mode)** > "Notifications du menu" (Menu notifications): "affichées" (shown, the default), "compteur seul" (counter only: the pills, no messages) or "coupées" (off: neither friends pill nor messages; the downloads and messages pills stay: they count what waits, they do not announce it); in the last two, news of a game with friends still shows.
  Kept in `<données>\medias.json`, field `notices`.
- **Waiting messages**: right of the friends online, the **messages pill** (the message bubble in blue, the number in its blue capsule, "99+" beyond 99) counts the unread messages from your friends and groups and the mentions of your nickname in rooms; a muted conversation does not count, and nothing shows when nothing waits: a zero would tell nothing to someone walking past the cabinet.
  It sits in the corner of the tables menu and on the gear's line of full screens (system menu, My badges, help…); a rising number lands with a light bounce.
  On a cabinet, the player card's bar gets shorter to leave it room, without covering the brand.
  A click opens **"Messages en attente" (Waiting messages)**, subtitled "3 messages attendent votre lecture" (3 messages are waiting to be read): one line per conversation, never one per message, the most recent first.
  Each line gives the friend's nickname, the name of the group or room, its kind ("Message privé" (Private message), "Groupe" (Group), "Salon" (Room)) and the last message received, preceded by its author in a group or room; on the right, "2 non lus" (2 unread; "2 mentions non lues", 2 unread mentions, in a room) and the time, or the date for another day.
  A muted conversation stays in the list, last, greyed, with "Sourdine" (Muted).
  Start opens the conversation where it lives (the friend's thread in Messages, the group chat, the room), and its "Retour" (Back) comes back here; once read, it leaves the list and the pill drops accordingly.
  A friend removed or a group left no longer shows there.
  Below, under "Communauté" (Community), the shortcuts: Messages, Groups, your country's room, Friends and Recent activity, each with its detail, a mark on Groups or Friends when an invitation or a request waits there; their "Retour" (Back) also comes back here.
  "Retour" (Back) returns to the tables menu, or to the system menu when the pill was clicked from a full screen.
  Guest mode: neither pill nor screen.
  Code: `app/src/inbox-menu.ts` (`createInboxMenu`, `inboxCount`, `inboxOrder`), the conversations gathered by `inboxThreads` and the shortcuts by `inboxLinks` in `app/src/frontend.ts`, the mentions per room by `mentionThreads` in `app/src/rooms-menu.ts`; the pill drawn by `inboxPill` in `app/renderer/frontend.js` (`.inbox-pill` in `frontend.css`); tests: `packages/core/src/test/messages-attente.test.ts`.
- **Status corner pills**: each opens the screen it announces, from the wheel as from the System menu and the other full-screen pages where the corner also is: the player card opens "My level", the badges pill "My badges", the friends online pill "Mes amis" (My friends), the downloads pill the manager.
  Their "Back" returns to where you came from: the wheel, or the full-screen page as it was, chosen entry included (`openFromCorner` in `app/src/frontend.ts`, `library.interrupt`).
  Nothing opens over a message or a confirmation waiting for its answer, nor during a long operation or while typing.
  Tests: `packages/core/src/test/entete-homogene.test.ts`.
- **Texts**: a 24 px title and a 20 px detail, each on one line: names and titles shortened at the source (40 and 56 characters at most), then to the width really left (the detail first loses its last segments, then its last words, with "…"; the start, which carries the meaning, stays).
- **Code**: the queue, rules, texts and snapshots in `packages/core/src/notices.ts` (`NOTICE_RULES`, `createNoticeCenter`, `socialNotices`, `downloadNotices`); the wiring in `app/src/menu-notices.ts` (network and download-queue snapshots, clock, redraw only when the notification changes) and `app/src/frontend.ts` (`zoneScreen`, `currentZone`, the rule being `noticeZone` in `packages/core/src/noticezone.ts`); the rendering in `app/renderer/frontend.js` (`cornerBox`, `noticeToast`, `fitNotice`) and `frontend.css` (`.status-corner`, `.friends-pill`, `.notice-toast`).
  A new source only adds a kind to `NoticeKind`, its rule and texts, and its snapshot.
- **Tests**: `packages/core/src/test/notices.test.ts` (one at a time and on time, grouping, summary, priority, expiry, nothing when the screen does not allow it, delay before repeating, snapshots without the startup state, every kind translated and fitting its line with long names, the screen's area read in `frontend.ts`, banner and notification without repetition, texts at the 20 px floor).
